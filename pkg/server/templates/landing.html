Health Scoring

A–F grades across demand, throughput, and responsiveness. Bus factor and pony factor surface single-contributor and single-company risk before it becomes your incident.

Activity & Trends

Monthly PRs, reviews, issues, and forks visualized over time. PR size distribution shows review bottlenecks. Issue open/close ratios detect growing backlogs.

Velocity

Time to first response, lead time to merge, change failure rate, and release cadence. Know how fast work moves through the pipeline and whether releases are stable.

Quality

PR review ratio, review latency, and contributor reputation scoring. Surface PRs that need extra scrutiny and measure whether code is getting proper attention.

Community

Contributor retention, momentum, and first-time contributor funnel. See which companies are contributing and whether newcomers are converting to repeat maintainers.
