Terms of Service

Please review and accept the following terms before using DevPulse.

Data Collection & Access

  • DevPulse collects and analyzes publicly available data from GitHub repositories you add to your dashboard.
  • Data collected includes contribution events (pull requests, issues, reviews, comments), repository metadata (stars, forks, language, license), releases, and contributor profiles.
  • Private repository data is only accessible when you explicitly grant access through a GitHub App installation.

Data Storage & Use

  • Imported data is stored in our database and used solely to generate project health analytics and insights for your dashboard.
  • Your data is isolated from other users through row-level security. Other users cannot view your tracked repositories or analytics.
  • We do not sell, share, or distribute your data to third parties.

Your Controls

  • You can add or remove repositories from your dashboard at any time.
  • You can revoke DevPulse access by uninstalling the GitHub App from your organization or account settings.
  • You can sign out and clear your session at any time from the dashboard or via /auth/reset.

Usage Limits

  • Free accounts are limited to 5 tracked repositories and 2,000 imported events per week.
  • When weekly limits are reached, data imports are paused until the following week.
  • Higher limits are available on paid plans.
